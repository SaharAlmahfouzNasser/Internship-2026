
# Agentic AI Internship 2026 – Student Selection Projects

This public repository showcases the projects completed by students as part of the selection process for the **Agentic AI Internship 2026**.

Each project demonstrates the applicant’s ability to understand a problem, design an AI-based solution, implement and evaluate the proposed approach, and clearly communicate the results. The projects may cover topics such as large language models, autonomous AI agents, multi-agent systems, retrieval-augmented generation, computer vision, machine learning, and workflow automation.

The repository aims to:

* Recognize the effort and creativity of the participating students.
* Provide examples of practical Agentic AI applications.
* Encourage knowledge sharing and collaboration.
* Serve as a learning resource for students interested in AI research and development.
* Document the technical work completed during the internship selection process.

Each project folder contains the student’s implementation, documentation, and instructions for running the project, where applicable.

The projects are shared publicly for educational and research purposes. The ideas, code, and documentation remain attributable to their respective student contributors.
